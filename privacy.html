Privacy

IdealForms builds and runs online forms for businesses and individuals. This page explains what we collect on this website, and how we handle the answers people give in the forms we build for our clients.

On this website

When you ask for a quote, we collect your name, email address and the details you send about the form you need. We use this only to reply to you and to build your form.

We use Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, to see how many people visit and which pages and links bring them here. It uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, the site that referred you, your approximate location and your device type. It does not tell us who you are. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on.

In forms we build for clients

When you fill in a form we built for a business or an event organizer, that organizer is responsible for your information. We act on their behalf. We store responses so the form works, send them to the organizer and the tools they choose, and fix problems when they come up.

  • We do not sell form responses or use them for our own marketing.
  • Questions about a response you sent, including requests to see or correct it, should go to the organizer who sent you the form. We will help them answer.
  • Payments in forms are processed by Stripe, directly into the organizer's own account. We never see or store full card numbers.

Where information is stored

Forms run on a third-party form platform, and this website runs on a third-party hosting service. These providers may store information outside Canada, where it can be subject to the laws of that country. We choose providers that protect information to a standard comparable to Canadian law.

How long we keep it

  • Business forms: responses are kept while the plan is active. When a client cancels, we send them a full export and delete the responses within 30 days.
  • Personal forms: responses are kept for the form's live period, then deleted within 30 days after the organizer has their export.
  • Quote requests to us: kept for up to two years so we can follow up on your project, then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we have to keep it by law. To make a request, use the form on our home page and mention privacy in your message. We reply within 30 days.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
