06 / Questions

Before you ask.

Who owns the responses?

You do. Responses go straight to your email and your own Google Sheet or tool. You can request a full export at any time.

Do I need to learn any software?

No. We build and maintain the form. You just read the responses wherever you already work.

Can it go on my existing website?

Yes. It can sit inside a page, open as a pop-up from a button, or live on its own link. The examples above show all three.

Will my form show your branding?

Business forms carry no IdealForms branding. Personal forms show a small “Powered by IdealForms” note on the thank-you screen, after the form is sent, never on the form itself.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Keeping your form live on your own web address with your branding, small edits when your questions or prices change, a check that everything still works, and a monthly summary of responses and drop-off.

What counts as a small edit?

Changing questions, options, prices, text or where responses are sent. Up to 30 minutes a month is included. Bigger jobs, like a new form or a new integration, are quoted first.

What happens if I cancel?

Business plans have a 3-month minimum, then you can cancel any time. When you cancel, the form goes offline at the end of your billing period and you get a full export of every response.

Who is the Personal plan for?

Personal events and groups: party RSVPs, reunions, club sign-ups, team rosters and one-off surveys. Anything that takes payments, uses your business name or address, or needs ongoing changes is a Business form.

Are there payment processing fees?

Stripe charges its standard card fees to your own Stripe account. We add nothing on top.
